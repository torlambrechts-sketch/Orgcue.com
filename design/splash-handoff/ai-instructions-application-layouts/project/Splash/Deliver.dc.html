03

A system prompt for teams without developers.

Put the whole specification into Claude Projects, ChatGPT Enterprise or a Custom GPT as a single prompt: role, context, invariants, rules per area, must-nots, examples and output format. XML or Markdown, with a token count. Names and personal data are left out.

<system_instructions>
  <role>You are the assistant for Northwind CRM…</role>
  <hard_invariants>
    <rule id="REQ-DATA-04">Customer data stays in the EU.</rule>
  </hard_invariants>
  <negative_constraints>
    <rule>Do not send e-mail without a rep.</rule>
  </negative_constraints>
</system_instructions>
