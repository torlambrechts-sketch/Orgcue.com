03

Let stakeholders vote on the MVP. See the spread, not just the average.

Reviewers upvote, downvote, propose a phase and comment. You see how far apart they are: three up and three down is very different from no votes at all. Disputed features go straight to Conflicts.

  • Votes count equally; the owner decides
  • Protected features: downvotes are advice only
  • Turn any feature into an interview question
