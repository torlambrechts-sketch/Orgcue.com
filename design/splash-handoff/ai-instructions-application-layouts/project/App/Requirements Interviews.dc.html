BOARD DIRECTION · 21 OCT 2026Ask every stakeholder the questions only they can answer. Let them rate, skip and move on.Requirements interviews for AI coding projects. One interview per person, one section per screen, MoSCoW on every question. The answers become a prioritised backlog and a CLAUDE.md a coding agent can build from.
Open the builder →Take the interview as Omar, customer success
AREASAll ten: functional, data, security, integrations, UX, adoption, performance, compliance, operations and AI behaviour.
STAKEHOLDERSEleven default roles for a CRM, from sales rep to executive sponsor. Add any person or role.
IMPORTANCEMoSCoW on every question. Won't becomes an explicit out-of-scope line for the coding agent.
QUESTION TYPESFree text, yes / no, multiple choice, rate a proposed requirement, user story.
FLEXIBILITYAI proposes questions from a bank and the brief. The owner edits, assigns roles and decides what is sent.
FORMATOne section per screen. Skipping takes one tap and never needs a reason.
What the board said
LVLena Voss · AI lead«Ask about behaviour, not prompts. A sales rep can say what the AI must never send. Nobody can write a system prompt in a survey.»
MHMarcus Hale · Developer, builds with Claude Code«The output that matters is a CLAUDE.md with codes. If the agent cannot cite REQ-SEC-02, the requirement does not exist.»
KHKaren Holt · Requirements engineer«Where you can, have people rate a proposed requirement instead of answering an open question. Rating is fast; writing is where people drop off.»
TETom Ericson · Security architect«Security also rates the AI items. If sales says Must and security says Won't, that is a conflict to decide, not a number to average.»
ARAna Ruiz · Project manager«One section per screen keeps it under ten minutes. Always show how many sections are left.»
PNPriya Natarajan · Change and adoption«Skipping must carry no guilt. A skip is data too: it tells you that you asked the wrong person.»
Open questions for you · defaults in place Should external customers answer anonymously? Default: they answer under their name, and only the owner sees it. Who signs off the backlog?
