# OrgCue competitive landscape: strategy execution, PPM and work management

Research date: 9 November 2026. Method: web search only. Direct fetches of vendor sites were blocked by the session's network policy (tested once on cascade.app, which failed). Most facts come from search summaries of vendor pages, press releases (Business Wire), vendor docs and changelogs, and review or aggregator sites (G2, Capterra, GetApp, Gartner Peer Insights, Tability, rfp.wiki). Vendor claims are self-reported.

Conventions:
- **unverified** means only a weak, single or third-party source supported the item, or it comes from general product knowledge and no search result in this session confirmed it.
- Packaging lists tier **names only**. No prices are recorded on purpose.
- Dates are shown where sources gave them. Third-party pricing and plan pages often conflict, and those conflicts are noted.

## 1. Landscape summary

### 1.1 Strategy execution, OKR and KPI

The segment is consolidating and moving to AI. **WorkBoard bought Quantive on 28 May 2025.** Quantive is no longer sold on its own, quantive.com now redirects to workboard.com, and customers are being moved over. **Microsoft retired Viva Goals on 31 December 2025.** That freed up Microsoft-centric OKR customers, and Profit.co, WorkBoard and others are openly going after them. The rest of the field splits into four groups:
- **Enterprise strategy-execution platforms:** WorkBoard, Cascade and Profit.co. They sell AI agents such as a "chief of staff", alignment scoring and generated business reviews.
- **Public-sector and mission-driven scorecard tools:** ClearPoint, Spider Impact and i-nexus (Hoshin). They focus on the balanced scorecard, strategy maps, KPI reporting and board reports.
- **OKR tools for SMB and mid-market:** Perdoo, Mooncamp, Weekdone and Tability.
- **HR performance suites where goals are one module:** Betterworks, Lattice and Leapsome.

The big work platforms now ship native goals: Asana Goals, Atlassian Goals inside Focus and the Strategy Collection, ClickUp Goals, Planner Goals and ServiceNow SPM OKRs. That pushes standalone OKR tools upmarket into strategy, KPIs and portfolio. The two clearest signals are WorkBoard's AI-native SPM launch and Atlassian's Focus.

### 1.2 Programme, project and portfolio management (PPM/SPM)

Gartner's 2025 SPM Magic Quadrant (4 Aug 2025) named **Planview, Broadcom Clarity and Bizzdesign (Alfabet)** as Leaders. ServiceNow SPM and Shibumi were also included. In the APMR Magic Quadrant, monday.com, Planview (AdaptiveWork) and Planisware were Leaders in 2025. For 2026 (3 Aug 2026), Planview and Planisware say they are Leaders again and Wrike is the only Visionary.

Consolidation continues:
- **Planview bought Sciforma** (18 Feb 2025).
- **Wrike launched an SPM product "powered by Shibumi"** (14 Jul 2026).
- **Smartsheet launched vsActual** at ENGAGE 2026, an AI-native product that tracks plan against actual for strategy execution.

Every enterprise PPM vendor now ships an AI assistant or agents:
- Planview Anvi (Oct 2025)
- Planisware Oscar
- Clarity Vaia, plus configurable agents and MCP from 16.4
- ServiceNow Now Assist for SPM agents
- Workfront AI Collaborators and MCP
- Microsoft Planner's Project Manager agent

The core of the category is still demand, scoring, scenarios, capacity by role, time-phased financials, stage gates and benefits.

### 1.3 Work and task management

The leaders all repositioned in 2025 and 2026 as **"AI work platforms" built around agents**:
- **Asana:** AI Studio, then AI Teammates (GA April 2026), then the StackAI acquisition (May 2026).
- **monday.com:** monday magic, vibe and Sidekick (Jul 2025), then monday Agents (2026).
- **ClickUp:** Super Agents (Dec 2025), then Brain² (Jun 2026).
- **Notion:** 3.0 Agents (Sep 2025), then Custom Agents (2026).
- **Linear:** Linear Agent (Mar 2026).
- **Atlassian:** Rovo agents, which reached Standard plans in late 2025.
- **Airtable:** relaunched as "AI-native" with Omni and Field Agents (Jun 2025).
- **Wrike:** AI Agents GA (Feb 2026).

MCP servers are now standard, so external agents such as Claude, Copilot and ChatGPT can read and write work data. Even Basecamp (Basecamp 5, 2026) added an agent-ready API, a CLI and Skills while avoiding native generative AI. AI pricing is moving to credits or consumption: monday credits, ClickUp Super Credits, Airtable credits and Smartsheet vsActual. Asana, monday, Smartsheet and Wrike keep moving up into portfolio and resource management, and add-ons for timesheets, budgets and capacity planning are spreading.

## 2. Vendor notes

### 2.1 Strategy execution / OKR / KPI

**Cascade**
- Positioning: strategy execution platform for strategy offices, with global hosting in the US, EU and AU.
- Signature features:
  - Strategy Map module with multiple maps at company or department level.
  - Pre-defined strategy models and frameworks.
  - Plans with objectives, projects and measures.
  - KPI integrations.
  - Dashboards and reports.
  - Alignment scoring of initiatives against priorities.
- 2025–26 additions: **Tapestry AI**, which Cascade says powers the whole platform. It includes:
  - AI Strategy Intelligence, which checks execution against intent.
  - AI Risks & Dependencies, which flags blockers early.
  - AI Business Context, which pulls updates from meetings, emails and files.
  - AI Reports & Meetings, which produces decision-focused meeting packs.
  - AI-calculated real-time alignment scores.

  Most modules are marked "New". Release dates were not found.
- Enterprise and platform: SSO on Enterprise (SAML), with setup handled by Cascade. Hosting choice of US, EU or AU. SCIM was not found (**unverified**). API and integrations exist (**unverified** detail).
- Packaging (names conflict by source): Cascade support lists Planning, Essentials and Enterprise. The pricing page shows a free Plan, Essentials and Enterprise. G2 lists Free Team, Essentials and Enterprise+.

**WorkBoard (now including Quantive)**
- Positioning: enterprise strategy execution and OKR leader. Says the Quantive deal combines "the top two" enterprise OKR players. Co-sells with Microsoft and Workday.
- Signature features:
  - OKRs with alignment.
  - Scorecards (plan vs actual, leading and lagging measures).
  - Running Business Reviews with auto-generated summaries.
  - Smart meeting agendas.
  - Kanban action plans.
  - Deep Microsoft Teams app and a Microsoft 365 Copilot agent.
  - Power BI usage.
- 2025–26 additions:
  - WorkBoardAI agents: Reporter Agent; Digital Chief of Staff and Leadership Coach (GA 2025, usage-based pricing).
  - Expanded Microsoft 365 Copilot agent.
  - **AI-native Strategic Portfolio Management** with a Portfolio Analyst Agent (launch date not confirmed).
  - In Sept 2025 WorkBoard reported 40 large enterprises migrating from Quantive.
- Enterprise and platform: enterprise account required for the Teams app. SSO, SCIM, audit and API are expected at this tier but were not confirmed (**unverified**).
- Packaging: not published, quote only.

**Quantive (formerly Gtmhub; StrategyAI / Results)**
- Status: **acquired by WorkBoard on 28 May 2025.** The standalone product is no longer sold, and quantive.com redirects to workboard.com (third-party, Jul 2026).
- Historic features:
  - More than 170 data integrations for automatic KPI and KR updates.
  - Insightboards.
  - OKR process-health reports.
  - AI OKR drafting (2023).
  - "Singularity" forecasts of goal attainment (2023).
- Relevance: its connector-driven KPI model and forecasting set the bar many still compare against.

**Perdoo**
- Positioning: OKR, KPI and strategy (Strategic Pillars) for growing companies.
- Signature features:
  - OKRs, KPIs and Initiatives.
  - Strategic Pillars.
  - Goal map / alignment.
  - Board view (formerly KPI Boards).
  - Check-ins.
  - Engagement, Performance and Custom dashboards with export.
- 2025–26 additions:
  - AI Coach drafts KRs, Initiatives (beta, Aug 2025), KPIs and OKRs, and reviews or drafts Strategic Pillars.
  - KPI comparison with the prior period or year (Sep 2025).
  - Cumulative KPI totals and dashboard export for meetings (Oct 2025).
  - Milestone target type.
  - Integrations: Trello, HubSpot, Google/Outlook calendar, monday.com, Snowflake.
  - **Perdoo 3.0** with a unified UX and performance work (date not confirmed).
- Enterprise and platform: superadmin feature toggles for AI. SSO and API exist (**unverified**).
- Packaging: Free, Premium, Supreme (official support page). There is a contract minimum on paid plans.

**Profit.co**
- Positioning: broad "AI-powered strategy execution platform" covering OKR, KPI, Balanced Scorecard, Hoshin Kanri, PPM, tasks, performance reviews and recognition. Explicitly markets itself as a Viva Goals replacement.

### 2.2 PPM / programme management

**Planview (Portfolios, AdaptiveWork; ex-Sciforma)**
- Positioning: largest SPM vendor. Gartner SPM Leader (2025, and again 2026 per a June 2026 press release). APMR Leader with AdaptiveWork in 2025 and 2026.
- Portfolios, signature features:
  - Demand management and prioritisation.
  - Investment and capacity **scenarios**.
  - Time-phased **financial planning** (targets, budgets, forecasts vs actuals, costs and benefits).
  - Capacity planning by role.
  - OKRs cascaded portfolio → programme → team.
  - Roadmaps.
- AdaptiveWork (ex-Clarizen): project, resource and financial management; timesheets; billing and expenses; stage-gate, agile or hybrid delivery.
- 2025–26 additions:
  - **Planview Anvi** (14 Oct 2025): Copilot renamed Anvi. Copilot Chat became **Anvi Chat** and Copilot Workflows became **Anvi Agents** (Nov 2025).

  - Planview says customer data is never used to train generative AI.
  - Sciforma acquired 18 Feb 2025. One third party says it was rebranded "Planview ProjectAdvantage" (**unverified**).
- Enterprise and platform: SSO, roles, audit, API and integrations including Jira, ServiceNow and ERP are standard for the vendor (detail **unverified** this session).
- Packaging: quote only; tier names not found.

## 4. Trends for 2025 to 2026

1. **Agents are now table stakes, and MCP is the interop layer.**
   - Every leader shipped named agents in 2025–26: Asana AI Teammates, ClickUp Super Agents and Brain², monday Agents, Notion Custom Agents, Linear Agent, Wrike Agents, Rovo, WorkBoard Chief of Staff, Profit.co Athena, Planview Anvi Agents, Clarity agents, ServiceNow Now Assist agents, Workfront AI Collaborators, Microsoft PM agent.
   - MCP servers let external agents (Claude, Copilot, ChatGPT, Codex) read and write work data at Wrike, Asana, monday, Linear, Atlassian, Teamwork, Basecamp, Clarity, Workfront and Smartsheet.
   - "Bring your own agent as a team member", with permissions, audit and human checkpoints, is the 2026 pattern (Wrike, Workfront).
2. **AI moves from summarising to running governance.** The new features read updates, flag drift, draft status and run reviews:
   - drafting status and initiative updates (Linear, WorkBoard Reporter)
   - generating business reviews and meeting packs (WorkBoard, Cascade)
   - plan-health linting (ClearPoint)
   - drift detection against plan (Smartsheet vsActual)
   - anomaly detection (Planisware)
   - scenario generation (Planview Anvi)
3. **Strategy, portfolio and work are converging into one product.**
   - Strategy vendors are moving into portfolio: WorkBoard SPM; Profit.co with a PPM module; Cascade alignment scoring.
   - Work platforms are moving into strategy and portfolio: Asana Goals and Portfolios with budgets and timesheets; monday portfolio; Smartsheet Portfolios and vsActual; Wrike SPM (Shibumi); Atlassian Strategy Collection (Focus + Talent + Align); ClickUp Goals.
   - This is exactly OrgCue's thesis, and the large vendors are doing it by acquisition or bundling.
4. **Consolidation and retirements.**
   - WorkBoard acquired Quantive (May 2025).
   - Planview acquired Sciforma (Feb 2025).
   - Bizzdesign absorbed Alfabet (Jan 2025).
   - Wrike partnered with Shibumi to power its SPM (Jul 2026). Shibumi's ownership status is **unverified**.
   - Asana acquired StackAI (May 2026).
   - Microsoft retired Viva Goals (Dec 2025), moved Project for the web into Planner (Aug 2025) and is retiring Project Online (Sep 2026).
   - Atlassian retired Atlas, which became the Goals and Projects platform apps (2025).
   - Together these create migration demand: ex-Viva Goals, ex-Quantive and ex-Project Online customers.
5. **Packaging is shifting to credits and consumption for AI**, with suite collections on top:
   - credits: monday, ClickUp Super Credits, Airtable, Smartsheet vsActual, usage-priced WorkBoard agents, ServiceNow usage measurement
   - collections: Atlassian Teamwork and Strategy Collections; Workfront Select, Prime and Ultimate plus a Planning add-on
   - Wrike added Apex and retired Enterprise.
6. **Capacity and financials move downmarket.** Asana's Timesheets and Budgets add-on, Smartsheet Portfolios with Scenario Planning, ClickUp Gantt baselines and Planner Plan 3 baselines and critical path bring classic PPM depth to work tools. Real benefits realisation, capex/opex and stage gates still sit mostly with Planview, Clarity, ServiceNow, Planisware and Shibumi.
7. **Goal management stays split between HR and strategy.** Betterworks, Lattice and Leapsome tie goals to performance and talent: Talent Intelligence, Lattice Habits, Leapsome AI goals. Strategy tools tie goals to KPIs and initiatives. Customers increasingly want both, linked through HRIS.
8. **Trust features matter more.** Buyers increasingly expect:
   - data residency (US, EU, AU, JP, UK)
   - "no training on customer data" statements (Planview, Teamwork, ClickUp)
   - ISO 42001 (ClickUp)
   - AI admin toggles (Perdoo, Leapsome, Teamwork)
   - agents that inherit user permissions (Workfront)
   - prompt-injection guards (ServiceNow Guardian)
