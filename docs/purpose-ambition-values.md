# Presenting ambition, purpose, values and value: five views for OrgCue

Research date: 9 November 2026.

## Read this first

- **The evidence is mostly secondary.** It comes from vendor help centres, vendor blogs and companies' own published documents. The research environment could not open several vendor sites directly, so some findings rest on search excerpts of those pages.
- **Some layouts are unconfirmed.** Where a layout could not be confirmed from text, this note says so.
- **The sample data is invented.** All numbers in the views belong to the fictional demo company, MosaicX.

## Summary

Strategy tools treat purpose and values as text, and they rarely connect them to anything measured:
- **Cascade and WorkBoard** hold vision, mission and values as text blocks above the plan.
- **Perdoo** puts one "Ultimate Goal" at the top of its strategy map, but has no values object.
- **People tools turn values into behaviour data.** Bonusly, Workhuman, Lattice and 15Five tag recognition posts with company values, and Culture Amp and Lattice heat-map survey items on values by team.
- **Annual reports present value created per stakeholder.** They often follow the Integrated Reporting value creation model and the GRI 201-1 "economic value distributed" table.

OrgCue's opportunity is to connect all of this to the strategy it already holds. The ambition is tracked against the north-star measure. Values carry their behaviours, recognition and survey scores. Value creation draws on the same objectives and initiatives.

## The five views

| # | View in OrgCue | The question it answers | Main references |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Ambition** (`/#st-ambition`) | Are we on track for the 2026 ambition, and what closes the gap? | Amplitude North Star Framework; Collins & Porras BHAG (HBR 1996); Perdoo Ultimate Goal; WorkBoard scenarios |
| 2 | **Purpose and values** (`/#st-purpose`) | Why do we exist, and what do our values look like in practice? | Sinek's Golden Circle (2009); Collins & Porras core ideology; Netflix, Amazon and HubSpot behaviour-based values |
| 3 | **Values in action** (`/#st-kudos`) | Are people living the values? | Bonusly, Workhuman, Lattice and 15Five value-tagged recognition; Culture Amp and Lattice survey heat maps |
| 4 | **Value creation** (`/#st-value`) | What value do we create, and for whom? | Integrated Reporting value creation model (IIRC 2013, revised 2021); GRI 201-1; stakeholder "value created" pages; EBITDA bridge |
| 5 | **V2MOM** (`/#st-v2mom`) | One page from vision to measures | Salesforce V2MOM (Benioff, 1999); Cascade V2MOM template |

All five are under **Strategy → Purpose and value** and in their own group in the Templates gallery. Every control works, and changes are kept in the visitor's browser.

### 1. Ambition: a north-star tracker

**What you see**
- **Ambition statement:** shown with the north-star measure (recurring revenue) and the share of the way travelled from the 2023 baseline.
- **Four facts:** what was required by today on a straight path to the ambition; where the current pace lands in 2026; where it lands with the selected initiatives; how many quarters are left and the pace needed.
- **Chart:** actual revenue against the straight path, the current-pace forecast and the forecast with selected initiatives.
- **The three ambition goals:** recurring revenue, retention and margin.
- **"Close the gap" table:** tick initiatives on or off to see their effect on 2026.

**Why this design**
- **Amplitude** separates one outcome metric from the inputs teams can move.
- **Collins and Porras** say a BHAG needs a clear finish line, and the research notes their model "doesn't supply" intermediate milestones. The straight-path line and the quarterly pace supply them.
- **WorkBoard and Planview** compare scenarios. The initiative ticks are a simple version of that.

### 2. Purpose and values: Golden Circle and values written as behaviours

**What you see**
- **Golden Circle:** a clickable diagram (Why, How, What) with editable statements.
- **Mission, vision and ambition cards:** labelled "core ideology · timeless" or "envisioned future · 2026", following Collins and Porras.
- **One card per value**, each with:
  - a one-line meaning;
  - "We do" and "We don't" behaviours;
  - a champion;
  - how well it is lived (from the values pulse) and how often it is recognised;
  - a "Recognise someone for this" button.

You can edit, add and delete values. A renamed value carries through to recognition and the survey.

**Why this design**
- **Netflix** said that "the real company values … are shown by who gets rewarded, promoted, or let go". **Amazon and HubSpot** write principles as observable behaviour.
- **Sinek's circle** is memorable but has no measures, so the cards add the evidence.

### 3. Values in action: recognition and the values pulse

**Recognition tab**
- **Feed:** posts tagged with a value (giver, receiver, message, likes).
- **Give recognition:** a dialog for writing a new post.
- **Filters:** by value, giver or receiver.
- **Side panels:** recognition by value, and the most-recognised people.

**Values pulse tab**
- **Heat map:** one survey statement per value, scored by business unit, with the average and change since last quarter.
- **Controls:** switch the survey quarter; switch to "difference from company average".
- **Hot spots:** the three places where values are least lived.

**Why this design**
- **Bonusly** charts recognition by value and has per-value leaderboards. **Workhuman** reports "Core Values Received", where a value nobody receives may need a clearer definition.
- **Culture Amp** heat maps show each group as a difference from a baseline and advise checking response rates first. The view shows the response rate.
- **One statement per value** makes values separable, which a generic "we live our values" question cannot.

**Design caution from the research:** leaderboards can reward popularity, so the view shows the most recognised people modestly and leads with the stories.

### 4. Value creation: stakeholders, value bridge and model

**Value for stakeholders**
- **Four cards:** clients, people, owners and society, each with three measures and their change.
- **Donut:** how the value earned is shared (employees, suppliers, reinvestment, taxes, owners, communities), following the GRI 201-1 table.
- **Negative changes are shown in red**, for example voluntary turnover, as Integrated Reporting asks for erosion as well as creation.

**Value bridge**
- **Waterfall:** operating profit from 2023 to the 2024 forecast, and from 2024 to the 2026 ambition (£10.8M, which is 18% of £60M).
- **Bars:** green adds value, red reduces it.

**How we create value**
- **Integrated Reporting flow:** the six capitals as inputs; the business model with the purpose and values at its centre; outputs; outcomes per stakeholder.

**Caution from the research:** "value distributed" is dominated by supplier payments in many companies. Bridge driver definitions vary, so a method note belongs in any real board version.

### 5. V2MOM

**What you see:** five stacked sections.
- **Vision:** editable.
- **Values:** ranked, and reorderable with arrows.
- **Methods:** ranked and reorderable, each with an owner, initiative, linked measures and status. Click to edit; add new ones.
- **Obstacles:** each linked to the method it blocks, with a resolve toggle.
- **Measures:** pulled live from the strategy objectives. Click one to open it on the strategy map.

**Why this design**
- **Salesforce** numbers methods in priority order, and places values between vision and methods so they decide conflicts between methods.
- **The research's main criticism** is that V2MOM is "a document, not a dashboard". OrgCue fixes that by drawing measures from live objectives.

## Research findings by reference

1. **WorkBoard "WoBo Strategy".**
   - A long-range strategy module holding a narrative, mission, vision, values, beliefs or trends, and attachments.
   - A "poster-ready" strategy page shows pillars, outcomes, assumptions and the investment plan.
   - Values are static text. It is an enterprise add-on.
   - Sources: help.workboard.com/en/articles/13341227-introduction-to-wobo-strategy (excerpt); zoftwarehub.com/products/wobo-strategy/overview.
2. **Perdoo.**
   - One "Ultimate Goal" (mission and vision) sits at the top of the Strategy Map, with 3 to 5 Strategic Pillars below and OKRs and KPIs in lanes.
   - Pillars can be reordered and archived.
   - No values object was found.
   - Sources: support.perdoo.com/en/articles/3112288-strategy-map; /2998444-set-up-your-ultimate-goal-mission-vision; /4725666-strategic-pillars.
3. **Cascade.**
   - The Strategy Model starts with Plan Principles: Vision and Values by default, with Mission or custom blocks optional.
   - Values can be shared company-wide.
   - Templates exist for "Vision Mission Values" and V2MOM.
   - Where vision and values live has moved between Planner, Teams and Plan Settings.
   - Sources: support.cascade.app/understanding-the-cascade-strategy-model; help.cascade.app/add-values; cascade.app/templates/v2mom-template.
4. **Salesforce V2MOM.**
   - Vision, Values, Methods, Obstacles, Measures; created by Marc Benioff around 1999.
   - Methods are ranked. V2MOMs cascade, are public inside Salesforce, and are renewed at least twice a year.
   - Sources: salesforce.com/blog/how-to-create-alignment-within-your-company/; trailhead.salesforce.com (V2MOM modules); lattice.com/articles/v2moms-how-they-work-and-when-to-use-them.
5. **Amplitude North Star Framework.**
   - One north-star metric representing customer value, with 3 to 5 input metrics beneath it (breadth, depth, frequency, efficiency) and work below those.
   - Drawn as a top-down tree, and as dashboard templates.
   - Sources: amplitude.com/books/north-star/about-north-star-framework; amplitude.com/blog/product-north-star-metric.
6. **Value-tagged recognition.**
   - **Bonusly:** up to 12 value hashtags; analytics by value; per-value leaderboard.
   - **Workhuman:** award reasons map to values; Culture Hub "Our Culture" tab; Core Values Received analytics.
   - **Lattice:** values on praise; a full-screen Praise Wall.
   - **15Five:** # value tags in High Fives with a hashtag filter.
   - Sources: help.bonus.ly/en/articles/357165-company-values-hashtags; bonusly.com/post/analytics-dashboard; help.lattice.com/en-us/articles/16542381-display-your-public-praise-wall; success.15five.com/hc/en-us/articles/16767917040539.
7. **Survey heat maps.**
   - **Culture Amp:** each group shown as a difference from the company score (green or red), a "Compared to" control and a Heatmap Explorer.
   - **Lattice:** an Alignment theme and delta comparison.
   - Sources: cultureamp.com/blog/heatmapping-your-engagement-results; support.cultureamp.com/en/articles/12853780-heatmap-comparisons; help.lattice.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500001406181.
8. **Golden Circle.**
   - Simon Sinek, TED talk and *Start With Why* (2009).
   - Three concentric circles, Why inside How inside What.
   - Memorable, but has no measures and is often criticised as over-simplified.
   - Sources: mindtools.com/abfhq53/start-with-why.
9. **Collins & Porras, "Building Your Company's Vision"** (HBR, September–October 1996).
   - Core ideology (core values and core purpose) is to be preserved.
   - The envisioned future (a 10 to 30 year BHAG and a vivid description) is to stimulate progress.
   - Sources: jimcollins.com/pdf/Vision_Framework.pdf.
10. **Values as behaviours.**
    - **Netflix:** the 2009 culture deck, rewritten in June 2024.
    - **Amazon:** 16 Leadership Principles, two added on 1 July 2021.
    - **HubSpot:** Culture Code (2013), HEART values, a trade-off ranking.
    - Sources: jobs.netflix.com/culture; aboutamazon.com/working-at-amazon/our-leadership-principles; blog.hubspot.com (culture code).
11. **Integrated Reporting value creation model.**
    - Framework published in December 2013 and revised in January 2021 (adding purpose, and "creation, preservation or erosion").
    - Six capitals; inputs → business activities → outputs → outcomes.
    - Sources: integratedreporting.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/InternationalIntegratedReportingFramework.pdf.
12. **Stakeholder value pages and bridges.**
    - "Value we create" sections at Alliander, BASF, ABB and Unilever.
    - GRI 201-1 economic value generated and distributed.
    - EBITDA bridges as waterfalls. Private-equity value bridges are criticised for misleading percentages.
    - Sources: globalreporting.org (GRI 201); abb.com/global/en/company/about/value-creation-model; ludovicphalippou.substack.com/p/should-value-bridges-be-criminalized.

## What could come next

- Link recognition and the values pulse to people's reviews, as Netflix, Amazon and HubSpot do.
- Cascade V2MOMs to departments, with each department's methods linked to the company methods.
- Replace the sample bridge drivers with figures from the finance system, with a method note.
